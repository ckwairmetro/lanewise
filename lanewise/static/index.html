See how you really play.

Lanewise reads your recent ranked solo games and compares every part of your game with players at your rank, in your role, on your champions. It shows where you spend your time on the map, which champions are working for you, and the habits that move your results.

  • Skill breakdown: laning, farming, fighting, survival, vision, objectives and teamplay as percentiles against players like you.
  • Heatmap: where you are in each phase of the game, and where you take and lose fights.
  • Champion pool: what to keep, what to drop, counters to watch and similar champions to try.
  • Traits and habits: your play style, tilt after losses, long sessions and loss streaks.
